## What it is

ROSS is an art access tool for blind users. It conveys visual content through touch, temperature, and sound.

## The problem

A museum audio guide describes a painting in words. That tells you what the guide noticed, not what is in front of you.

## What makes it interesting

It was a finalist at Hack the North 2025. Using temperature as a channel is unusual and gives the user a sense that is not already occupied by audio.

## What you can learn

Accessibility design is often a mapping problem. The question is which senses are free and which properties fit them.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the hardware or any testing with blind users.
