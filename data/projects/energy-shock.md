## What it is

Energy Shock is a fast action game built at Global Game Jam 2018. It was named PC Gamer's best free game of the week and later entered Steam Early Access.

## What makes it interesting

The jam build was good enough to be recommended as a free game, which gave the team an audience before the commercial version existed.

## What you can learn

A finished small build is a marketing asset. Releasing it free can do more for a project than holding it back until launch.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the mechanics or how the Early Access version changed the jam design.
