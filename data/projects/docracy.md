## What it is

Docracy was an open-source platform for legal documents, described at the time as GitHub for contracts. Users could publish, compare, and reuse agreements.

## The problem

Standard contracts were locked inside law firms and template sites. Small companies needed the same documents but could not afford the drafting.

## What makes it interesting

It won a TechCrunch Disrupt hackathon in May 2011 and raised $650,000 from First Round Capital months later. The pitch was simple enough to land in a weekend.

## What you can learn

Borrowing a metaphor from a known tool is a fast way to explain a new one. "GitHub for contracts" does more work than a feature list.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not cover the licensing model, the quality of the documents, or why the company wound down.
