## What it is

Superfluid is a protocol for streaming money on-chain. Payments flow per second instead of arriving as discrete transfers.

## The problem

Salaries, subscriptions, and vesting are all continuous in concept and discrete in practice. A monthly transfer is an approximation that creates friction and disputes.

## What makes it interesting

It started at ETHOnline 2020 and became a widely used DeFi primitive. Streaming is a base layer that other applications can build on, which is why it persisted.

## What you can learn

Changing the unit of a payment changes the products built on it. Per-second money makes a different set of applications possible.
