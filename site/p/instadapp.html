InstaDApp

Decentralized finance middleware platform that simplifies interactions with lending protocols like MakerDAO through a unified interface.

Fintechdefiblockchainethereum

ETHIndia - 2018

Official

Brothers Sowmay and Samyak Jain built a prototype that collapsed MakerDAO's 12-step loan process into one action. They won ETHIndia 2018 and grew the project into a major DeFi infrastructure platform.

Evidence
