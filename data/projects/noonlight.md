## What it is

Noonlight is a personal safety service. A user triggers an alarm from a phone or a connected device, and a dispatch center responds.

## The problem

Calling emergency services requires speaking, knowing where you are, and having time. In a dangerous situation, none of those may be available.

## How the experience works

The user raises an alarm from the app or a hardware button. The service contacts emergency responders with the user's location and details, without the user having to hold a call.

## What makes it interesting

It began as a Startup Weekend project in 2013 and became a service integrated into dating apps and wearables. The integrations matter: safety is more useful when it sits inside the app where the risk happens.

## What you can learn

A safety feature that requires opening a separate app competes with panic. Putting it in the surface people already use removes that step.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the dispatch integration, false-alarm handling, or the hardware devices.
