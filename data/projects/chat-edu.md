## What it is

ChatEDU is a study assistant that turns course material into answers, practice sets, and flashcards. It accepts PDFs, slides, notes, and lecture video.

## The problem

Students often have the material but not enough practice questions. Making them by hand is slow, and generic question banks do not match the course.

## How the experience works

A student uploads the material and asks questions. Answers cite the source passage, and the same content can be turned into practice problems or cards.

## What makes it interesting

Four Vanderbilt undergraduates built it for a Microsoft AI hackathon in 2023 because they lacked practice material for an algorithms final. It took first place for the Technical Project Prize.

## What you can learn

The best hackathon project often solves the builder's own week. You already know the user, the workflow, and whether the result is good.
