## What it is

Connected Earth Museum is a virtual museum about the climate, hosted by an AI guide. It presents climate data as 3D and 2D exhibits.

## What makes it interesting

It won the Art and Technology award at NASA Space Apps 2024. The entry treats a dataset as an exhibition, with a host and a route through the material.

## What you can learn

A guided narrative can make scientific data approachable without simplifying it. The host does the work a curator would.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not link a demo or describe the AI host. Whether the exhibits are accurate or the guide stays grounded is unknown.
