The Roottrees are Dead

Deduction puzzle game where players research a 1998 candy-company inheritance to fill in an intricate family tree.

Gamespuzzledetectiveresearch

Global Game Jam 2023 - 2023

Official

Jeremy Johnston built the prototype during GGJ 2023 (theme: Roots). After eleven months of polish it shipped free on itch.io, then got a full Steam remake in January 2025.

Evidence

  • Created for a Global Game Jam by Jeremy Johnston in under a week in 2023: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Roottrees_Are_Dead
  • Originally a Global Game Jam project cobbled together in less than a week in 2023; later rebuilt with hand-drawn art: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/686651/roottrees-ai-original-illustrator-replacement
  • Steam page states the game was originally created for Global Game Jam 2023 and released as a free browser game: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2754380/The_Roottrees_are_Dead/
