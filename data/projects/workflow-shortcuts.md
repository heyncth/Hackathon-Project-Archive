## What it is

Workflow is a visual automation app for iOS. Users chain actions into a script and run it with one tap.

## The problem

Phones can do many things, but each task requires a sequence of app switches. Automation lets a user define the sequence once.

## What makes it interesting

Ari Weinstein started it after MHacks 2014 at the University of Michigan. Apple acquired it in 2016 and turned it into Shortcuts.

## What you can learn

A hackathon project can become a platform feature. The acquisition was a shortcut to distribution no independent app could match.
