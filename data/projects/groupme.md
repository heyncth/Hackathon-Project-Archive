## What it is

GroupMe is group messaging for phones. You create a group, add people, and everyone sees the same thread. The app runs on smartphones, and members without one can join over SMS.

## The problem

Group texting in 2010 was a patchwork. Carrier group MMS behaved differently across devices, and adding someone mid-thread often broke the thread. A group that mixed smartphones and basic phones had no shared space.

## How the experience works

A message sent from the app reaches every member as a message in the group, and replies return to the same thread. The SMS path matters because it removes the requirement that everyone installs the app before the conversation can start.

## What makes it interesting

Jared Hecht and Steve Martocci built it at the TechCrunch Disrupt New York hackathon in 2010. The product decision that carried it was treating SMS as a first-class client rather than a fallback.

## What you can learn

A communication product is only as useful as its least-equipped member. Supporting the oldest channel in the group can be the feature that makes the rest work.
