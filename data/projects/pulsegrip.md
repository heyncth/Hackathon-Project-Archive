## What it is

PulseGrip is a motorized hand assist for people with paralysis. It reads muscle signals and closes the hand.

## The problem

Grip is one of the first functions lost after a spinal injury or nerve damage, and it is needed for almost every daily task.

## What makes it interesting

It was a finalist at Hack the North 2024. The device uses EMG, so the user's remaining muscle signal becomes the control input.

## What you can learn

Assistive hardware should use the signal the body still produces. Asking for a new input method adds friction to every use.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the grip force, the fit, or any user trial.
