## What it is

TowerFall is a local multiplayer archery game. Players fire arrows at each other in single-screen arenas, where one hit ends a round and arrows can be picked up after they land.

## The problem

Local multiplayer in 2012 was mostly party games built for casual play. TowerFall aimed at something faster and more precise, with the tension of a fighting game and the readability of a platformer.

## What makes it interesting

It started at the Vancouver Full Indie Game Jam in 2012. It became the first commercial release from Maddy Thorson, who later made Celeste.

## What you can learn

A jam build can be the whole design. TowerFall's rules fit on an index card: one hit, limited arrows, pick them up. The depth came from arena layout and movement, not from added systems.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record documents the jam origin and the designer, not the netcode or the changes between the jam build and the commercial release.
