## What it is

Live Faces was a live-streaming prototype built at a Facebook hackathon in 2012. It demonstrated broadcasting from the platform and is credited as an influence on Facebook Live.

## What makes it interesting

A hackathon demo can become a product direction years later. The prototype proved the interaction before the infrastructure and the business case existed.

## What you can learn

Some prototypes are arguments, not products. Their job is to make a decision possible, and they succeed when someone remembers them at the right moment.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the streaming stack, the audience, or how directly it influenced the shipped product.
