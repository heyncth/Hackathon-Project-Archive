## What it is

Neutun Labs built a seizure monitoring platform on a smartwatch. The watch detects seizure-like activity and logs it for the wearer and their clinician.

## The problem

Seizures are often unwitnessed. A patient's own report is unreliable, and a diary misses events that happen during sleep.

## What makes it interesting

It won the Pebble prize for best smartwatch app at Hack the North 2014 and continued development after the event. The project used a consumer watch rather than custom medical hardware.

## What you can learn

A general-purpose device can be a clinical sensor if the signal is good enough. That trade changes the cost and the adoption path.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the detection algorithm, its sensitivity, or any regulatory clearance.
