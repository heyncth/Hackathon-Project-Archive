## What it is

Sekai is a story-building platform that uses AI to help people create and remix interactive stories. It runs on Story Protocol, which handles licensing of the underlying intellectual property.

## The problem

A collaborative story has an ownership problem. If several people contribute characters and worlds, nobody can easily license what they made or get paid when someone else builds on it.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best of ETHDenver 2024. The project pairs a consumer-facing creative tool with on-chain licensing, which is harder than either piece alone.

## What you can learn

Creative tools need a reason to be on a chain. Here the reason is attribution and licensing, not payments for their own sake.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the AI pipeline, the licensing terms, or how a contributor's share is calculated.
