Ushahidi

Open-source crisis-mapping platform that crowdsources and maps eyewitness reports via SMS and the web.

Civic TechNetSquared Mashup Challenge winnercrisis-mappingcrowdsourcingopen-sourcemaps

Kenya post-election crisis rapid build - 2008

OfficialSource

Built in a few days by an ad hoc group of technologists - a crisis-response rapid build rather than a formally organized hackathon.

Evidence

  • Born out of the 2008 Kenyan post-election violence as an ad hoc rapid software build: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2010/aug/11/ushahidi-crowdmap-crisis-mapping
  • Initial deployment January 2008; won the NetSquared mashup challenge May 2008: https://www.ushahidi.com/about/blog/ushahidi-1-year-later
  • Founded in 2008; built in days by four technologists: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ushahidi
