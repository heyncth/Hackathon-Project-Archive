## What it is

Frame is a museum app that lets a visitor photograph an artwork and get information about it. It was a co-winner at the Walters Art Museum's first Art Bytes hackathon in 2012.

## The problem

Museum labels are fixed to walls. A visitor who wants context for a piece has to find the right plaque or wait for a tour.

## How the experience works

The visitor takes a photo. The app matches the artwork and returns information about it, using the phone as the label.

## What makes it interesting

It shared the top prize with Tanzaku, another entry from the same event. Both projects used the phone as the interface to the collection rather than building a separate guide.

## What you can learn

A museum already has the content. The hack is the lookup, not the encyclopedia.
