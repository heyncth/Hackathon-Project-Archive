## What it is

TakeOne is a robotic film studio. It plans shots, moves the camera and lighting, and edits the footage.

## The problem

A small production has a crew problem. A single operator can hold a camera or a light, not both, and coverage is what separates a professional look from a home video.

## What makes it interesting

It was a finalist at Hack the North 2026. The project treats the whole shoot as one system, from planning to edit, rather than automating a single camera move.

## What you can learn

Robotics in creative work is a workflow problem. The robot has to fit the way a shoot already runs.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the hardware or the planning model.
