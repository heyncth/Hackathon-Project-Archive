## What it is

HoneyPause is a marketplace for security reviews of on-chain projects. It connects protocol teams with auditors and white-hat researchers.

## The problem

Smart contracts hold funds from the day they deploy. A team that wants a review has to find a reviewer, agree on scope, and trust the result, often under time pressure.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best of ETHDenver 2024. The project is infrastructure for trust rather than another application, which is a harder pitch in a hackathon full of demos.

## What you can learn

A two-sided marketplace has to solve the cold start for the scarce side. Here that is the auditor's time, not the protocol's demand.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the escrow model, the reputation system, or whether any audits were completed.
