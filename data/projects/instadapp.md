## What it is

InstaDApp is a middleware layer for decentralized finance. It gives users one interface for lending protocols such as MakerDAO instead of separate positions in each.

## The problem

Each DeFi protocol has its own interface and its own way of managing collateral. A user with positions in several places has to watch all of them separately.

## What makes it interesting

Brothers Sowmay and Samyak Jain built it at ETHIndia 2018 in Bengaluru and won the hackathon. The product became a way to manage a portfolio of positions rather than a single protocol.

## What you can learn

Abstraction over competing protocols is a durable position. The user wants the outcome, not loyalty to one venue.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the smart contract architecture or how the platform handled risk across protocols.
