## What it is

Immersive Rehab is a virtual reality platform for rehabilitation. It targets neurological conditions where movement practice is part of recovery.

## The problem

Repetitive exercises are boring, and bored patients stop. VR can make the repetition tolerable and give the therapist data.

## What makes it interesting

It was featured by Red Bull Basement. The project sits at the intersection of two hard markets, clinical validation and consumer hardware, which is why so few rehabilitation products reach patients.

## What you can learn

In clinical VR, the therapist is a user too. A product that ignores their workflow will not be adopted, however good the headset demo is.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the exercises, the clinical evidence, or the regulatory path.
