## What it is

ODIN is a marketplace for smart contract circuit breakers, bonded through EigenLayer. Protocols can buy protection that triggers when a contract misbehaves.

## The problem

A bug in a deployed contract can drain funds before a team can react. An automatic stop is only credible if someone has money at stake on the trigger being correct.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best of ETHDenver 2024. Bonding the breaker to an economic stake is the design idea: the protection is only as good as the collateral behind it.

## What you can learn

Security claims need a cost for being wrong. A guarantee with no skin in the game is a promise.
