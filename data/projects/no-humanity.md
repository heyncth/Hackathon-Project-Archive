## What it is

No Humanity is a multiplayer survival game built to be extremely difficult. It has drawn more than 20 million players across platforms.

## What makes it interesting

It started at a Global Game Jam. Difficulty became the marketing: a game that most players cannot finish produces clips, complaints, and streams.

## What you can learn

Frustration is a shareable emotion. If the failure is fair and funny, players will advertise the game for you.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the mechanics, the platforms, or how retention held after the initial spike.
