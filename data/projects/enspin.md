## What it is

ENSPin keeps files referenced by ENS names available on IPFS. It pins the content so the link does not break when the original host stops serving it.

## The problem

A name that resolves to content is only useful if the content still exists. Pinning is the difference between a permanent record and a dead link.

## What makes it interesting

It won first place in the ENS Infrastructure category at ETHGlobal Taipei 2025. The project works on the unglamorous half of decentralization: persistence.

## What you can learn

Naming and storage are separate problems. A naming system does not guarantee that anyone keeps the file.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the pinning service, the cost model, or how it decides what to keep.
