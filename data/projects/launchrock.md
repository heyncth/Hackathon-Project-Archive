## What it is

LaunchRock built pre-launch landing pages that collected email addresses before a product shipped.

## The problem

A team with no product still needs a way to measure interest. A landing page with a signup form is the cheapest test of demand.

## What makes it interesting

It placed second at Startup Weekend Philadelphia in 2011, raised seed funding, and was acquired about three years after launch. The product was a page and a form, which is a small surface for a company.

## What you can learn

Sell the waitlist before you build the product. If nobody signs up, you saved a year.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the page builder, the pricing, or the acquisition.
