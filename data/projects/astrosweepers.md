## What it is

AstroSweepers is a concept for removing orbital debris. It proposes a way to clear space junk from busy orbits.

## The problem

Defunct satellites and fragments stay in orbit for decades. Each piece is a collision risk for working spacecraft.

## What makes it interesting

It won the Galactic Impact award at NASA Space Apps 2025. Debris removal is hard for reasons beyond engineering: someone has to pay for it, and no one owns the junk.

## What you can learn

A cleanup project needs a business or treaty answer before it needs a spacecraft. The hard part is often who authorizes the removal.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the capture mechanism, the target orbit, or any prototype. It is an award listing for a concept.
