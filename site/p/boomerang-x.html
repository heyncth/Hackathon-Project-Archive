Boomerang X

Momentum-based first-person shooter built around a single teleporting boomerang; started as a 48-hour GMTK Game Jam entry and shipped via Devolver Digital in 2021.

GMTK Game Jam - Game Maker's Toolkit

OfficialDemo

DANG! turned a weekend GMTK jam entry about throwing and chasing a boomerang into a full Devolver-published shooter. The studio later closed in 2023 after failing to fund its next game.

Evidence

  • itch page states the jam build was made in 48 hours for the GMTK game jam: https://dangstudios.itch.io/boomerang-x
  • Commercial release July 8, 2021, developed by DANG! and published by Devolver Digital: https://store.epicgames.com/p/boomerang-x
  • Follow-on studio news confirming Boomerang X as DANG!'s released title grown from the jam prototype: https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/boomerang-x-developer-dang-is-closing-after-failing-to-find-funding
