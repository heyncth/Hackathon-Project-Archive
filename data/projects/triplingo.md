## What it is

TripLingo is a translation app for travelers. It combines phrasebooks, slang, and a live translator.

## The problem

A phrasebook gives you correct sentences you will not hear back. A traveler needs to understand the reply, including the informal speech a textbook omits.

## What makes it interesting

It started at Startup Weekend Atlanta and grew into a company. The product treated slang and culture as part of the language problem.

## What you can learn

Translation is two problems, speaking and understanding. Most early apps solved only the first.
