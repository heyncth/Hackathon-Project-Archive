## What it is

The King is Watching is a roguelite where production only happens while the king is looking. Crops grow and miners work under his gaze, and stop when he looks away.

## How the experience works

The player decides where to direct attention. Since one gaze cannot cover the whole kingdom, every run is a choice about what to neglect.

## What makes it interesting

It was a top-rated entry at Ludum Dare 54 and later sold over 600,000 copies on Steam with tinybuild publishing.

## What you can learn

A single restriction can be the whole design. "Only while watched" generates every decision without extra systems.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe how the attention meter is tuned or what the full release added.
