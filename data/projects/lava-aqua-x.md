## What it is

LAVA AQUA X is a portable electric washing machine designed to use less water. It was built by the team LYLO.

## The problem

Laundry is a large share of household water use, and portable machines often trade efficiency for size.

## What makes it interesting

It won the Global Workshop at Red Bull Basement 2020. The project treats water use as a design constraint from the start rather than an afterthought.

## What you can learn

A sustainability claim needs a measurable baseline. The winning pitch is stronger when it states liters per load, not "less water".

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not include the water savings, the prototype status, or any testing.
