## What it is

Loot Crate was a subscription box for geek and gamer merchandise. Each month shipped a themed collection of items.

## The problem

Fandom merchandise was scattered across stores and conventions. A curated box turns discovery into a recurring purchase.

## What makes it interesting

It started at Startup Weekend Los Angeles in 2012 and grew to millions of subscribers. The business depended on licensing and logistics, not on software.

## What you can learn

A subscription is a promise about next month. The churn risk sits in the second box, not the first.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not cover the box economics, the licensing deals, or the company's later decline.
