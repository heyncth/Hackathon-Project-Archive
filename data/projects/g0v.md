## What it is

g0v is a Taiwanese civic-tech community that builds open tools for government transparency: budget visualizations, legislative trackers, referendum monitors, and epidemic dashboards.

## The problem

Government data is published, but not in a form most people can read. Budgets arrive as documents; legislative activity arrives as records without a view.

## How the experience works

Volunteers take public data, clean it, and publish it as a browsable tool. Each project is independent, and the community meets at recurring hackathons rather than through a single organization.

## What makes it interesting

It started with a late-2012 hackathon that produced a better national budget visualization. The portfolio grew to more than 40 hackathons, with tools for legislative monitoring, referendums, and COVID-19 data.

## What you can learn

Civic tech spreads through reusable artifacts. A budget parser or a data format lasts longer than a campaign, because the next team starts from it.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not cover funding, project governance, or how volunteers maintain tools after the hackathon.
