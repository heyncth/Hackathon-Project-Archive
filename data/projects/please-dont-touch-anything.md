## What it is

Please, Don't Touch Anything is a puzzle game about a console with one button and a note telling you not to press it. Each press changes the room and the puzzle.

## What makes it interesting

Four Quarters developed it after a jam prototype. The premise is a single forbidden action, which is enough to carry a short game and a lot of curiosity.

## What you can learn

A prohibition is a prompt. Telling the player not to do something is often more effective than telling them what to do.
