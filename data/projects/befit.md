## What it is

BeFit is a social fitness app that rewards exercise with NFTs. It was described at the time as BeReal for pushups.

## The problem

Fitness apps struggle with retention. A reward that has resale value is one way to make a streak worth protecting.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best of ETHDenver and the Impact track at ETHDenver 2024. The design borrows a social format and attaches a crypto incentive, which is a common pattern at Ethereum hackathons and a hard one to keep honest.

## What you can learn

An incentive only works if the reward costs less than the behavior it buys. Token rewards tend to attract the people who value the token most, not the people who need the habit.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not report user numbers, retention, or token economics. There is no evidence the incentive worked.
