## What it is

Astrogenesis is an itinerary builder for planetary tourism. It assembles a trip concept from destination data and presents it as a plan.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best Mission Concept at NASA Space Apps 2023. The entry reframes mission data as consumer planning, which is a different audience from the usual dashboard.

## What you can learn

The same dataset can support a serious tool and a playful one. A tourism frame can teach a general audience where things are in the solar system.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not include a demo link or describe the data sources. How the itineraries are generated is unknown.
