## What it is

2plant is a planning app for farmers. It combines soil moisture and evapotranspiration data to estimate when a field is at risk and when planting is more likely to succeed.

## What makes it interesting

It won the Galactic Impact award at NASA Space Apps 2024. The entry takes two ordinary agricultural measurements and turns them into a timing decision, which is the decision a smallholder actually has to make.

## What you can learn

A satellite dataset becomes useful when it answers a question with a date attached. "Is the soil wet enough this week" is a better product than a soil-moisture map.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record has no demo link and no field validation. Accuracy, coverage, and whether the app was used outside the competition are unknown.
