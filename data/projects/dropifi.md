## What it is

Dropifi was a customer communication platform, and the first African company accepted into the 500 Startups accelerator.

## What makes it interesting

It started at Startup Weekend Accra in 2011. The accelerator acceptance is the milestone the record preserves: a weekend project in Ghana reaching a Silicon Valley program.

## What you can learn

A local startup scene needs a visible path out. One accepted application gives the next team a reference point.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record covers the origin and the accelerator, not the product. Its features, customers, and eventual outcome are not documented in the linked source.
