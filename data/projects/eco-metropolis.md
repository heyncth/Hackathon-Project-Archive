## What it is

Eco-Metropolis is a city-building game that uses real environmental data. Players grow a city while the simulation responds to actual conditions.

## What makes it interesting

It won Most Inspirational at NASA Space Apps 2024. A game is a way to let someone experience a dataset as consequences rather than as a chart.

## What you can learn

If your data describes a trade-off, let the user make the trade. A simulation teaches the constraint faster than an explainer.
