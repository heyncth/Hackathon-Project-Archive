## What it is

The Box is decentralized physical infrastructure for peer-to-peer package delivery. It combines custom hardware with on-chain coordination.

## The problem

A package moves through a courier network that the sender does not control. A peer-to-peer option could use capacity that already exists, such as a neighbor already driving that route.

## What makes it interesting

It won the Hardware and IoT DePIN category at ETHGlobal Brussels 2024. The project is a physical device plus a protocol, which is a much harder demo than software alone.

## What you can learn

DePIN projects live or die on the hardware cost. A box that has to be manufactured and shipped changes the economics of the network.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the lock mechanism, the chain integration, or any pilot.
