## What it is

What's New? is a machine learning pipeline that estimates the probability of a Carrington-class solar storm.

## The problem

A storm of that size would damage satellites and grids. It is rare, so any estimate has to handle a heavily imbalanced dataset.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best Use of Science at NASA Space Apps 2022. The project frames the task as a probability rather than a yes-or-no forecast, which suits a rare event.

## What you can learn

Rare-event prediction needs a calibration story. A model that says "probably not" every day is right most days and useless.

## Limitations of this analysis
