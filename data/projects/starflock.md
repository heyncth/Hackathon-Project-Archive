## What it is

Starflock tracks how wildfire smoke disperses chemically, using airborne measurement data.

## The problem

Smoke from a large fire travels far and changes composition as it goes. Communities downwind need to know what is arriving, not only where the fire is.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best Use of Data at NASA Space Apps 2022. The project follows a plume rather than a fire perimeter, which is a different question from most wildfire tools.

## What you can learn

Air quality is a transport problem. The interesting data is often between the source and the sensor.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the measurement sources or the dispersion model.
