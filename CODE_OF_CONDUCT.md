# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, findings, and evidence quality.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the archive's data quality and integrity.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Publishing false, fabricated, or deliberately misleading claims about projects.
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private or sensitive information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour
may be reported to the maintainers through the repository's issue tracker or
directly to the repository owner.

Maintainers will review and address reports in a fair and timely manner, and
may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and other contributions
that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
