## What it is

The Street View Trike is a pedicab with a camera mast, built to capture Street View imagery where cars cannot go, such as parks and cultural sites.

## The problem

Street View covered roads. It could not cover a footpath, a palace courtyard, or a university campus, and those places were a large part of what users searched for.

## What makes it interesting

Dan Ratner built it during a Google workshop weekend as a 20% project in 2008. It became a full fleet rather than a one-off demo.

## What you can learn

A data-collection product is limited by its vehicle. Changing the vehicle changes which world is addressable.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the camera rig or the scale of the fleet.
