## What it is

Greetings from Earth is an interactive visualization that treats the ocean as a climate garden. Users explore the sea as a system that needs tending.

## What makes it interesting

It won the Galactic Impact award at NASA Space Apps 2023. The metaphor does the teaching: a garden implies care and seasons, which a temperature chart does not.

## What you can learn

Metaphor is a compression scheme. A good one carries relationships the audience already understands.
