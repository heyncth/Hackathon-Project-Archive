## What it is

Landsat Connect is a web app for following Landsat satellites. It shows surface reflectance data and notifies users when a satellite passes over an area.

## The problem

Landsat data is open but not approachable. A researcher or land manager has to know which scene to request and when it was captured.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best Mission Concept at NASA Space Apps 2024. The pass notification turns an archive into a subscription, which is the difference between data being available and being used.

## What you can learn

Notifications are a product feature, not a nicety. A dataset that changes on a schedule is more useful when it tells you the schedule.
