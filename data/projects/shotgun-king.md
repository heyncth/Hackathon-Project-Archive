## What it is

Shotgun King: The Final Checkmate is a roguelike chess variant. The player commands the black king, who has a shotgun instead of a full army.

## How the experience works

Each floor presents a white chess formation. The king moves as a king, shoots in a line, and picks up cards that change the rules between runs.

## What makes it interesting
