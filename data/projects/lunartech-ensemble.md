## What it is

LunarTech Ensemble is a website and immersive game that visualizes Apollo lunar seismic data.

## The problem

The Apollo seismometers recorded moonquakes for years, but the data sits in archives that few people explore.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best Use of Science at NASA Space Apps 2023. Turning a seismic record into something a person can move through is a way to make an old dataset legible.

## What you can learn

Old missions still hold unused data. A new interface can be the contribution, without a new instrument.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not link the experience or describe how the seismic data is mapped.
