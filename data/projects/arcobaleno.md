## What it is

Arcobaleno turns astronomical images into sound. It maps two- and three-dimensional data to audio so that blind users can explore space imagery.

## The problem

Astronomy communication is almost entirely visual. A description of a nebula can tell you it is bright, but not where the brightness sits.

## How the experience works

Image properties such as position and intensity are mapped to sound parameters. Moving through the image changes what the listener hears, which gives spatial information without pixels.

## What makes it interesting

It won the Global Connection Award at NASA Space Apps 2023. The project treats accessibility as a translation problem rather than a text alternative.

## What you can learn

Sonification is not a fallback for images. It is a second channel that can carry information the visual version buries in color.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the sonification mapping or report testing with blind users. Both are essential to judging whether it works.
