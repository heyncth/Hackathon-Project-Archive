ChatEDU

Study assistant that turns PDFs, slides, notes and lecture video into citation-grounded chat, practice sets and flashcards; built by Vanderbilt students for a Microsoft AI hackathon.

Microsoft AI for Students Hackathon - Microsoft - 2023

Official

ChatEDU began as a cram-session tool for a Vanderbilt algorithms exam inside a Microsoft student AI hackathon. The team incorporated feedback from students across multiple universities, won several hackathons, and pursued it as a paid study product.

Evidence

  • Four Vanderbilt undergrads built ChatEDU for a Microsoft AI hackathon after lacking practice material for an algorithms final; later reached about $6,779 MRR and won five hackathons: https://www.sofarbot.com/stories/chatedu-vasco-singh-6704-mrr
  • Devpost summary of the student Azure AI education hackathon where ChatEDU took first place for the Technical Project Prize: https://info.devpost.com/customer-stories/ai-hackathons-on-devpost-2024
