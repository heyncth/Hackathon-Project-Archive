## What it is

Pony Island is a puzzle game disguised as a broken arcade machine. The machine is run by the devil, and the game keeps breaking its own rules.

## What makes it interesting

Daniel Mullins developed it primarily during Ludum Dare 31 in December 2014, in 48 hours, on the theme "Entire Game on One Screen". It later became a Steam release.

## What you can learn

The metafictional trick only works if the underlying game is playable. The joke is layered on a real loop, not instead of one.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the puzzle design or how the commercial version expanded the jam.
