## What it is

Terra Nil is a reverse city builder. The player restores a wasteland into a working ecosystem, then removes their own machinery and leaves.

## How the experience works

Buildings purify soil and water and reintroduce species. Once the biome sustains itself, the player recycles the equipment, which is the opposite of a normal builder's ending.

## What makes it interesting

It began as a Ludum Dare 2019 prototype and later released commercially, including on Netflix Games.

## What you can learn

Inverting a genre's goal changes the whole emotional register. The win condition is the absence of the player's work.
