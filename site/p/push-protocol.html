What it is

Push Protocol is a decentralized notification layer for Web3 applications. It lets a wallet or app receive messages without polling a chain.

The problem

On-chain applications have no native way to reach a user. A transaction confirmation or a liquidation warning has to be discovered by refreshing.

What makes it interesting

It started at ETHGlobal 2020 and later raised $10 million. Notifications are unglamorous infrastructure, which is why they are a durable position once apps integrate.

What you can learn

Every application stack needs a messaging layer. Owning the default channel is more valuable than owning one app.

Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the protocol design or how spam and cost are handled.
