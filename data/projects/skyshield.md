## What it is

SkyShield identifies risk zones for satellites from near-Earth objects and meteor streams. It updates in real time as orbital data changes.

## The problem

Satellite operators need to know when debris or a meteor stream will cross an orbit. The information exists, but it is spread across sources and hard to act on.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best Use of Science at NASA Space Apps 2024. The product is a risk map, which turns open orbital data into a decision surface.

## What you can learn

For operational users, a map beats a table. The value is in the aggregation, not the raw data.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the data sources or the update rate.
