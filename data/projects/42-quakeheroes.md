## What it is

42 QuakeHeroes is a deep neural network that finds seismic events in time-series data. It reads waveform recordings and marks where an event begins.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best Use of Technology at NASA Space Apps 2024. Detection is traditionally handled with hand-tuned thresholds and analyst review, so treating it as a learning problem changes what the system needs: labelled examples instead of tuned filters.

## What you can learn

A model is a way to encode the judgement of the people who labelled the data. The quality of the labels sets the ceiling, not the architecture.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the network, the training data, or the detection accuracy. It is an award listing, not an engineering report.
