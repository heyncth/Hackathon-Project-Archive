## What it is

Proof of Picture detects AI-generated images using C2PA provenance data. It is aimed at newsrooms checking images before publication.

## The problem

A generated image can look like a photograph. A newsroom needs a way to check origin without trusting the file's own metadata.

## What makes it interesting

It won the AI and C2PA category at ETH Warsaw 2023. The project leans on a standard for content credentials rather than a classifier alone, which is a more durable bet.

## What you can learn

Provenance is only useful if the camera or editor signs at capture. Detection without adoption is a partial fix.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the verification flow or how it handles images with no credentials.
