JARVIS

AI assistant app that uses computer vision to describe surroundings and non-verbal cues for blind and visually impaired users; won Best Hack for Health at Cal Hacks 10.0.

Cal Hacks 10.0 - UC Berkeley HKN - 2023

Official
Profile

A team of first-year Georgia Tech CS majors built JARVIS over a Cal Hacks weekend after a visually impaired family member inspired the idea. The multi-award winning prototype describes nearby people and cues rather than only text.

Evidence

  • Georgia Tech first-year CS majors built JARVIS at Cal Hacks 10.0 and won Best Hack for Health plus Best Use of Zilliz and Best Use of GitHub: https://news.gatech.edu/news/2023/12/11/cs-majors-win-best-hack-health-app-visually-impaired
  • College of Computing writeup: JARVIS uses open-source AI, ML and computer vision to give visually impaired users situational awareness; inspired by a visually impaired family member: https://www.cc.gatech.edu/news/cs-majors-win-best-hack-health-app-visually-impaired
