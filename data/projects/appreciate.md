## What it is

Appreciate is a property management platform that shares profits with property owners. It won Columbia Startup Weekend 2022 and later raised a $750K seed round.

## The problem

Property managers usually charge a fee regardless of how the property performs. Owners carry the downside while the manager collects either way.

## What makes it interesting

The model ties the manager's revenue to the owner's result. That is a business-model change, not a feature, and it is the kind of decision a weekend pitch can communicate clearly.

## What you can learn

A pricing model can be the product. If your incentive is visibly different from the incumbent's, the pitch explains itself.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not cover the size of the portfolio, the terms of the profit share, or how the seed round was used.
