## What it is

Quick Insurance is a mobile prototype for buying short-term coverage on a valuable item with a few taps.

## The problem

Insurance is sold in annual policies with forms and agents. A person who needs cover for an afternoon, a bicycle, or a camera has no easy option.

## What makes it interesting

It won the Grand Prize and €4,200 at the TechCrunch Disrupt Berlin 2017 Hackathon. The demo was the purchase flow, which is the part incumbents make painful.

## What you can learn

In insurance, distribution and paperwork are the product. Shortening the flow is the innovation.

The record does not describe underwriting, claims, or whether the product launched.
