## What it is

Evoland is an action-adventure game where the game itself evolves. Each upgrade unlocks a new visual style or mechanic, moving from monochrome Game Boy graphics to 3D.

## What makes it interesting

It won the "Evolution" theme at Ludum Dare 24 in 2012, chosen from more than 1,400 entries. The theme and the mechanic were the same thing, which is why the prototype read as a complete idea.

## What you can learn

When the theme can become the mechanic, the jam entry explains itself. The constraint is the design.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record covers the jam win and the commercial release, not the engine or how the graphical stages were produced.
