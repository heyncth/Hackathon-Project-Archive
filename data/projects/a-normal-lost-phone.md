## What it is

A Normal Lost Phone is a narrative game played entirely through a found phone. You read messages, look at photos, and piece together who the owner was.

## The problem

Story games usually ask you to watch a character. A phone asks you to snoop, which is a more uncomfortable and more engaging position.

## How the experience works

The phone is the interface. There is no map and no character to move. Progress comes from reading the apps and following the clues they contain.

## What makes it interesting

It was built at Global Game Jam 2016 in Angoulême and later released on iOS, Android, and Steam. The interface was already familiar to every player, so the game needed no tutorial.

## What you can learn

Using an interface people already know removes the cost of teaching it. The player spends their attention on the story instead of the controls.
