Profile

A last-minute pitch at Startup Weekend LA became a national talking-point startup in under a month, drawing celebrity investors and a board seat for a former eBay CEO.

Evidence

  • Within six hours of the 54-hour LA Startup Weekend in February 2011, the founders cooked up Zaarly and built the first prototype: https://www.entrepreneur.com/leadership/from-business-idea-to-successful-startup-in-three-months/220019
  • Zaarly raised $14.1M Series A from Kleiner Perkins and Sands Capital; Ashton Kutcher seed investor; Meg Whitman joined board: https://www.businessinsider.com/zaarly-raises-14-million-2011-10
  • Zaarly was little more than an idea when it won LA Startup Weekend three weeks prior to launch coverage: https://techcrunch.com/2011/03/09/want-someone-to-bring-you-a-beer-get-that-and-anything-else-from-zaarly
