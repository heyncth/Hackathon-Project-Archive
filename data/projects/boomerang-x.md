## What it is

Boomerang X is a first-person shooter built around one weapon: a boomerang that teleports you to wherever it lands.

## How the experience works

You throw the boomerang, then recall it. Movement and combat use the same object, so positioning is the attack. The arena is designed around the arcs the boomerang can take.

## What makes it interesting

It started as a 48-hour GMTK Game Jam entry and shipped through Devolver Digital in July 2021. A single verb carried both traversal and combat, which is why the jam version could demonstrate the whole game.

## What you can learn

If one mechanic can serve two systems, the prototype already contains the design. The rest is content.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record covers the jam origin and the release, not the level design or how the team tuned the boomerang.
