## What it is

Carousell is a mobile-first marketplace for secondhand goods. A seller photographs an item, adds a price, and publishes a listing in about a minute.

## The problem

Classifieds sites were built for desktop browsers and long forms. Selling a used phone required a camera, a cable, and patience, when the phone in the seller's pocket was already the best tool for the job.

## How the experience works

The listing flow starts with a photo and asks for the minimum: title, price, category. Buyers browse and message the seller. The short flow is the product.

## What makes it interesting

The team built the working demo in 54 hours at Startup Weekend Singapore in 2012, under the name SnapSell, and won first place. The name changed; the one-minute listing stayed.

## What you can learn

Removing steps from a listing flow increases supply, and supply is what makes a marketplace usable. The camera was the feature, not the form.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record covers the origin and the demo, not the trust and safety system or the payment model.
