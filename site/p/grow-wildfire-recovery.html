G.R.O.W.

User-friendly GIS mapping tool for wildfire recovery and observation.

Civic TechLocal Impactwildfiregisnasa-space-apps

NASA Space Apps Challenge - 2024

Profile

What it is

G.R.O.W. is a mapping tool for wildfire recovery. It gives residents and planners a view of damage and recovery work in their area.

The problem

After a fire, information is scattered across agencies and spreadsheets. People who lost property need one place to see what happened and what is next.

What makes it interesting

It won the Local Impact award at NASA Space Apps 2024. The entry targets a specific community rather than a global audience, which is why a local award fits.

What you can learn

A recovery tool has to be usable by people under stress. Fewer layers and clearer labels beat more data.

Limitations of this analysis

The record does not link the tool or describe its data sources or deployment.
