## What it is

Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime is a couch co-op game. Players move around a circular ship and man its turrets, engines, and shields.

## How the experience works

The ship has stations, and there are more stations than players. Coordination means deciding who leaves which post and when, which turns movement inside the ship into the puzzle.

## What makes it interesting

It started at Global Game Jam 2012 in Toronto and later released on major consoles. The jam version established the shared-ship idea.

## What you can learn

A co-op design is interesting when players cannot do everything at once. Scarcity of attention is the mechanic.
