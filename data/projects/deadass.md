## What it is

DeadAss is a wearable that detects emergencies and acts on the wearer's behalf. It can secure funds and contact the right people when the wearer cannot.

## The problem

An emergency response usually needs three things at once: detection, a decision, and money. A person in danger may be able to supply none of them.

## What makes it interesting

It won the AI Hardware category at ETHGlobal Bangkok 2024. Combining a wearable with on-chain funds is unusual: the device has to act without a confirmation step.

## What you can learn

A system that acts for the user must define what happens when it is wrong. False positives are a design problem, not an edge case.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the sensors, the detection threshold, or any false-positive testing.
