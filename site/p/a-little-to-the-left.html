A Little to the Left

Cozy tidying puzzle game about sorting household messes; began as a GMTK Game Jam 2020 entry and became a multi-platform commercial release.

Gamesgmtk-jampuzzlecozycommercialplayable

GMTK Game Jam 2020 - Game Maker's Toolkit - 2020

OfficialDemo

Anne Macmillan and Lukas Steinman's jam entry about putting things where they belong grew into a Max Inferno studio game published by Secret Mode across PC and consoles.

Evidence

  • Initially made for GMTK Game Jam 2020 (theme Out of Control, 48 hours) then expanded to a full game released November 8, 2022: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Little_to_the_Left
  • Original jam submission of A Little to the Left (demo) for GMTK Game Jam 2020: https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2020/rate/699181
  • Studio origin story: Annie and Lukas made their first game at GMTKJam 2020, formed MAX INFERNO in 2021: https://www.maxinferno.com/press
