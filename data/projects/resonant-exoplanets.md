## What it is

Resonant Exoplanets is an AI system for detecting exoplanets. It looks for the signals that a planet leaves in observations of its star.

## What makes it interesting

It won Best Use of Technology at NASA Space Apps 2025. Detection is a needle-in-haystack problem, which is why machine learning fits the data.

## What you can learn

In discovery work, the cost of a false positive is an astronomer's time. A model should rank candidates, not declare planets.
