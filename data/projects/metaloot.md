## What it is

Metaloot is a launchpad that connects games to the Flow blockchain. It gives game teams a way to launch on-chain assets and reach players.

## What makes it interesting

It was named a winner among ten finalists at ETHGlobal Bangkok 2024, competing for a $750,000 prize pool. The project targets game studios rather than end users, which is a different distribution problem.

## What you can learn

Infrastructure for creators only works if the creators already have players. The launchpad is a multiplier, not a source of demand.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the launch mechanics, the Flow integration, or any games that used it.
