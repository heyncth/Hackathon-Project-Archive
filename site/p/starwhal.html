What it is

STARWHAL is a local multiplayer game where narwhals duel with space lances. It supports up to four players on one screen.

How the experience works

Movement is momentum-based and hard to control. The fun comes from the collision of a clumsy body with a sharp point, which is why matches are short and loud.

What makes it interesting

It was created at the Carleton Ottawa site of Global Game Jam 2013, then released commercially and played over a million times across platforms.

What you can learn

Silly physics is a low-cost way to make a local multiplayer game memorable. The comedy is in the control, not the art.

Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the porting work or the level design.
