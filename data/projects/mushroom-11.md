## What it is

Mushroom 11 is a platformer where the player controls a spreading fungal organism instead of a character.

## The problem

Platformers are built on a body with a fixed shape. It jumps, it fits through gaps, it takes damage. A blob with no fixed shape needs different verbs.

## How the experience works

You shape the organism to move it through spaces, growing one side and losing the other. Progress comes from managing the mass rather than from timing a jump.

## What makes it interesting

It started at Global Game Jam 2012 at NYU and was shortlisted for an IGF award for design. The jam constrained the team to a mechanic that could be understood without a character or a story.

## What you can learn

Remove the body and the genre's assumptions go with it. A single unusual verb can carry a whole game if the level design keeps asking new questions of it.

## Limitations of this analysis

The sources establish the origin and the award shortlist. The growth and destruction rules, and how the team tuned them, are not documented in the linked material.
