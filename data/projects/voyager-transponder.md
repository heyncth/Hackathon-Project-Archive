## What it is

The Voyager Transponder is a 200-gigabit optical transponder prototype. Network engineers built it at a Facebook hackathon in 2016.

## The problem

Long-haul optical links are expensive because the equipment is proprietary and specialized. A cheaper, open design could lower the cost of backbone capacity.

## What makes it interesting

The prototype contributed to the Telecom Infra Project, an effort to open up telecom hardware. A hackathon build feeding a industry consortium is an unusual path.

## What you can learn

Internal hackathons can seed external standards work. The prototype becomes an argument for a different supply chain.
