## What it is

zkPow is a zero-knowledge system for proving work reputation without revealing the underlying data.

## The problem

A reputation score is only useful if it can be checked. Sharing the raw history to prove it exposes information the person may not want to disclose.

## What makes it interesting

It won the ZK and Privacy category at ETH Porto 2023. The project separates the claim from the evidence, which is the core promise of a proof system.

## What you can learn

Privacy is a feature of verification, not its opposite. A proof that reveals nothing can be more convincing than a document that reveals everything.

## Limitations of this analysis

The record does not describe the circuit, the reputation model, or the cost of proving.
